# QuestMod

**QuestMod** adds a classic-style quest system to Minecraft Forge 1.20.1: a **Quest Master** (NPC) that offers, takes in and rewards quests, with a clear panel instead of a giant tree, built to work with **any mod**.

## What it includes

- **Several quest types**: kill, break, consume, craft, smelt and deliver items. Objectives accept IDs from **any mod** and **tags** (`#minecraft:logs`).
- **Several objectives per quest**, several rewards with NBT, experience and **milestones with percentage rewards**.
- **Requirements and chains** of quests, **sections** (for example one per mod) and automation (auto-accept and auto-claim).
- **Community quests** with a server-wide counter, where each player decides whether to take part.
- **Customizable NPCs**: player skin, skin from a link, resource pack texture, the model of **another mob from any mod**, or an item with a 3D model.
- **Quest book** (key or item) to use it **without NPCs**, and **datapack** quests for modpacks.
- **In-game editor** with an item picker (inventory or the whole registry, mods included).
- **Customizable**: colors and **textures** through resource packs, and translatable to any language.
